### Slide 2: The Problem

Your organization has invested in getting data into Databricks. The tables are there. The governance is in place. But when someone asks a business question — "What's our revenue by region?" — three analysts give three different answers.

**The data is right. The definitions are missing.**

### Slide 3: The Insight

**Genie Ontology starts with UC Business Semantics — and it's easy to build with Genie Code.**

*Your Genie Ontology is the governed business context in Unity Catalog — Metric Views, domains, and definitions — that every Genie Agent, dashboard, and BI tool draws from.*

Unity Catalog Business Semantics lets you define your KPIs, business terms, and domain structure **once** — and have every dashboard, every Genie Agent, every notebook, and every BI tool use the same governed definitions.

Genie Code is the AI assistant that makes building this fast. It understands your data model, your metadata, and your lineage. It generates the definitions. You review and approve.

**The result: consistent, trusted answers — from day one.**

### Slide 5: How It Works

**Morning: Understand your data**

1. We research your industry's standard KPIs and learn how your organization talks about its domain
2. We analyze your data model — every table, every join, every relationship — using Genie Code's native UC awareness
3. We generate Metric View definitions with your business terminology embedded

**Afternoon: Build and validate**

4. We deploy the Metric Views to your dev environment and walk your governance team through the definitions
5. We create a Genie Agent and test it live — asking real business questions and validating the answers
6. We run benchmarks, discuss what's working, and hand off the repo

**You're involved at every step.** This isn't a black box — you see every decision, every definition, every line of YAML. That's deliberate: when you understand how it was built, you trust the answers it produces.
